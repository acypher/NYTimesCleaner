**News without autoplay**

Even though I pay for NYTimes, they keep popping up ads for their Family Plan and other subscription offers. I also dislike distracting videos that play automatically. NewsMinus stops both.

NewsMinus runs only on nytimes.com pages and works entirely on your device:

- Stops videos from playing automatically — press play only when you want to watch.
- Hides the Times' own subscription and upgrade promos that interrupt reading.
- No accounts, no analytics, no data collection of any kind.

Turn it on in Safari's extension settings, allow it for nytimes.com, and read the news in peace.
